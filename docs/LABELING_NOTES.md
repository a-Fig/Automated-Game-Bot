# Labelling notes

What things in Fractured Ground (T3, Bridgewatch area) are and aren't, for building
the training set. Entries marked "Tyler" are Tyler's corrections from 2026-11-04.

- **ore**: small dark rock outcrop with pale grey/white streaks, half-buried in sand
  (Tyler confirmed a detection at the top edge of the screen was real ore).
- **not fiber**: the big leafy yellow-green desert bush with spiky fronds and purple
  stems. It's decoration (Tyler). The model correctly ignores it; keep it as a
  negative example.
